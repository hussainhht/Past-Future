# Solo temporal ghost prototype

Each solo timeline can see a cyan, translucent hologram of the other character and a small area of their world. The Past sees the Professor and nearby Future geometry; the Future sees the Student and nearby Past geometry. The echo uses world coordinates: it stays where the inactive character was left, and follows them when you reposition them. Its area can leave the camera view naturally.

This is enabled by default in both playable solo rooms. Press **G** or **R1 / RB** to toggle it. Press **T** to switch roles, walk near the structure you want to observe, then switch back. This works in either direction. In the maintenance gallery, leave the Professor near the service equipment before operating the Past power controls. The passage to storage is clear in both eras.

The area extends 4.5 world units around the inactive character and fades toward its boundary. Their model idles at their last position and facing. Nearby opposite-timeline geometry refreshes when state, position or inspection changes; active mechanical animations update at up to 20 times per second. Settled geometry is cached. This includes power levers, the aged walkway, doors and the recorder cell. Laboratory props use their authored Past poses or displaced and rotated Future poses to match the hologram timeline. Past-only and Future-only geometry are filtered accordingly. Room labels and private record panels are not duplicated.

The echo stays available during equipment inspection and hides during pause, the journal, transitions and the chapter ending. Controlling the Professor shows the actual Future with the Student’s Past hologram; controlling the Student shows the actual Past with the Professor’s Future hologram. LAN games do not create the solo overlay.

`scripts/world/temporal_ghost.gd` creates mesh copies and an animated model without gameplay scripts, collision, interaction targets or audio. It reads the room's confirmed visual state and never submits puzzle actions. The radius, translucency and silhouette effect are defined in that script and `shaders/temporal_ghost.gdshader`.

Visible surroundings render in two combined meshes. The inactive character's material surfaces are combined into one skinned surface, preserving animation. A spatial grid selects nearby sources; large floors, walls and boundary objects are geometrically clipped to an inscribed 16-sided area before upload. Every submitted surroundings vertex stays within 4.5 units of the inactive character horizontally. This prevents a nearby corner of a room-sized mesh from submitting the whole mesh. The shader still provides the soft circular fade.

Clipped geometry is cached until the inactive character moves, the role changes, or the object changes pose. Old neighborhood copies and their cached geometry are released when they move away. When the entire echo leaves the camera view, its animation and geometry updates stop; returning to it refreshes confirmed puzzle changes immediately. Settled object transforms are not rewritten every frame. The overlay adds approximately 3–4 draw calls in the rendered review.

Run `godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/temporal_ghost.gd` to check uploaded vertex bounds, neighborhood eviction, offscreen suspension and catch-up, anchoring and facing in both directions, Future-first entry, both toggle inputs, live state changes, Past and Future prop transforms, inspection, pause and preservation of gameplay state. The maintenance test runner includes it. Set `TEMPORAL_GHOST_REVIEW_DIR` and use a graphics renderer to capture the actual ghost before and after restoring power, plus Past and Future holograms in both rooms.
