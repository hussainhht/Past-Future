# Earlier 2D student prototype artwork

The active character uses the detailed eight-direction cutout rig described in [student_2d/README.md](student_2d/README.md). This earlier three-view sprite sheet and its atlas resources are retained as an alternative and are not referenced by the active player scene.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then copied unchanged into `student_directions.png` (1536 × 1024 RGBA). The sheet contains front, back, and right-facing neutral poses; left-facing movement mirrors the right view. The game uses three AtlasTexture resources, normalized to a common visual height and foot baseline. Atlas regions exclude transparent padding; alpha cutouts suppress faint background alpha. These poses use a small procedural walking bounce, not a drawn walk cycle.

This is shared prototype artwork for both timelines. The older professor still needs separate art. Edit the atlas resources to change crop regions and `scenes/characters/student_player.tscn` / `scripts/characters/student_player.gd` to change presentation or movement.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game character directional sprite sheet on transparent background for a Godot 2.5D game. Create ONE PNG sprite sheet, landscape 1536 by 1024, with exactly THREE equal-width cells side by side (each cell 512 by 1024), no borders. Same single young adult university student shown in each cell: first cell FRONT view facing viewer, second cell BACK view facing away, third cell strict SIDE view facing RIGHT. Every character full body from top of hair to boots. Identical scale, identical total height, identical aligned foot baseline y=900, top of hair near y=130, horizontal center x=256 in each respective cell. Leave generous transparent gutters; no crossing cell boundaries. Flat hand-painted 2D cutout style, simple bold shapes and dark crisp outlines, slightly angular whimsical mystery adventure style, oversized head for readability at 90 pixels high, small stocky body, short tousled dark brown hair, warm tan skin, ochre brown student jacket with cream shirt and satchel strap across chest, charcoal trousers and chunky dark brown boots. Relaxed neutral standing pose with arms slightly away from torso, feet apart. Front has two simple dark eyes and a small nose, back shows back of hair and jacket without a face, right profile shows one eye and protruding nose facing RIGHT. Muted palette fits a painted low-poly laboratory. Keep details minimal and silhouettes clear. This is source game art rather than a presentation mockup. EXACTLY three depictions of the same character, FRONT BACK RIGHT in that order, no additional poses, no text, no labels, no decorations, no floor or ground shadow, no opaque background, no gradients outside silhouettes. True transparent alpha background.
