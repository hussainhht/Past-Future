# Four-view professor study

All four images in `../references/49/` were inspected directly before modeling.

- `frontview.png`: same large rounded head and compact body as the student. Swept charcoal hair with silver front/temple strands; heavy dark brows, brown oval eyes, a broad soft nose, a guarded closed mouth, short gray/brown beard, and subtle forehead/eye lines. Navy notched coat falls to the upper thigh. Slate collared shirt, charcoal/olive waistcoat with centered buttons, dark trousers, and black leather boots. Lower coat patch pockets, left breast pen pocket, and three visible coat buttons. No bag, strap, or student badge.
- `backview.png`: layered swept hair continues around the skull and gray lower nape. Coat has a central back seam, rear hem vent, worn lower edges, and sleeve damage. Coat silhouette narrows gently at the waist and widens toward the hem. Sleeves end above the wrists.
- `sideview1.png`: anatomical right side; visible upper-sleeve tears and scuffed cloth. Hair has real side/rear volume. The short beard follows cheek/jaw contours. Coat front and back extend below the waistcoat.
- `sideview2.png`: anatomical left side; worn analog watch on a brown strap, pen pocket, gray temple strands, and pointed lapel. Black footwear has a rounded toe and low sole.

The references vary slightly in sleeve tear placement, beard density, and hair-lock shape. The model keeps a single coherent coat, beard, and hairstyle across all views. Sleeve damage is concentrated on the right, and the watch/pen pocket remain on the left.

The shared rig/proportions preserve the connection to the student and existing animations. Coat tails blend to pelvis/upper legs; head details stay rigid to the head, and the watch follows the original left-arm weights. The source skeleton's names, axes, hand slots, and animation mapping are retained.

Review front/back/both sides and animated poses under actual Godot lighting. The reference studio lighting, softer sculpted forms, cloth surface detail, and exact facial proportions cannot be reproduced perfectly by solid-color procedural geometry; comparison images are retained to make those differences visible.
